Common questions

What people ask us before handing over a file

What happens to my files?

They are encrypted on arrival, kept on servers located in France, and deleted automatically 72 hours after the import ends. You can also delete them yourself at any time from the app. We never use your contacts for anything other than your import.

What if Transvase gets the column mapping wrong?

Nothing is imported until you approve it. The review screen shows the first five rows exactly as they will arrive at the destination, and every mapping can be changed from a drop-down. If you only notice afterwards, the seven-day undo puts the list back the way it was.

My platform is not on the list.

Any platform that accepts a CSV import or has an API works: Transvase produces a file in the exact format the destination expects, or sends the contacts straight over by webhook. Send us the name of the tool and we will confirm within 24 hours.

Am I still GDPR-compliant after an import?

The date and the source of consent travel with each contact whenever your file has them. When they are missing, Transvase says so plainly rather than inventing a date — it is your call whether those contacts go into the list.

How long does a list of 50,000 contacts take?

Reading and cleaning take about ten minutes. Verifying the addresses against the receiving servers, which is the slow step, runs around an hour and a half for 50,000 addresses. You get an email when the report is ready.
